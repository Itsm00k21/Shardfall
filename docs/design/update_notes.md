## v0.1 beta draft

Date: 2026-10-17. Headline: Haven is open. Clear Rootvault, roll a shard, and bring four players to the Heartwood.

### Player notes

- Five classes stand in Haven: Warden, Tidecaller, Forgeborn, Frostblade, and Voidcaster.
- Rootvault has Normal, Hard, and Nightmare, with a door choice between rooms. Grandmother Gnarl waits at the end.
- The Heartwood runs on Story and Normal. A party is 4 to 8. Everyone readies, then you share one server. Under 4, the raid refuses. Solo practice is the Practice Grove, and it pays no loot.
- Rolls show an Epic bar at 100 and a Legendary bar at 1,000. A refused roll prints the reason. Leaving the Roll tab clears the last card.
- Loading tips rotate. A raid hop shows one during the blackout, and a failed hop hides it.
- Five starter goals pay 10 Shard Keys each. The tracker sits on the HUD.
- You start with 250 gold, 40 Shard Keys, two chests, and three Healing Tonics. The first pet is not in the kit yet.
- Forge +1 is 150 gold and +2 is 300. Later steps keep the rising curve. +8, +9, and +10 each spend one Gnarl's Seed. A Rootvault clear pays 360 gold. A Heartwood clear pays 480. A finished daily or weekly pays 150.
- The weekly board prints the real quest line, with the real count.
