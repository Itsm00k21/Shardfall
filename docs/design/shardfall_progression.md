# Shardfall Progression Curve v1 (draft, Oct 16 2026)

## Layers (each gives a different "number goes up")
1. **Player level** (account): 1-60 at launch of all 5 realms; beta cap 20. Unlocks systems + realms.
2. **Class level**: 1-30 per class; unlocks talents (Lv 5/15/30) and class cosmetics. Shares 50% of XP with other classes after the first reaches 30 (catch-up).
3. **Gear power**: rarity (Common-Mythic) + upgrade level (+0 to +10 at the Forge) + sockets.
4. **Crafting level**: 1-50; unlocks recipes, better salvage, merge tiers.
5. **Collection**: Relic Sets per realm, Shard Codex (all shards found), pets. Small permanent account bonuses.
6. **Mastery (endgame)**: after max level, XP fills Mastery ranks giving tiny stat points + titles. Never-ending, small gains.

## Leveling pace (beta, realm 1)
| Level | Approx. total playtime | Unlock |
|---|---|---|
| 1-3 | 0-6 min (tutorial) | class, rolls, pet, chest |
| 5 | ~20 min | Lv5 talent, Hard Rootvault preview, daily quests |
| 8 | ~45 min | Crafting Lv tab, chest merging |
| 10 | ~1.5 h | Hard Rootvault, guild browsing, 2nd ring slot |
| 12 | ~2.5 h | Verdant boss, Relic Set chase |
| 15 | ~4 h | Lv15 talent, Nightmare Rootvault, weekly boss |
| 18 | ~6 h | Charm slot, Mythic merging |
| 20 (beta cap) | ~8-10 h | The Heartwood raid (post-beta), Mastery ranks |
XP curve: XP to next level = 100 x level^1.6 (smooth early, slows mid). Daily first clears give 3x XP (catch-up for casual players).

## Realm pacing (full game)
| Realm | Levels | First clear | "Finished" (full set) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verdant Wilds | 1-20 | ~8 h | ~20-30 h |
| Sunken Reach | 20-30 | +6 h | +20 h |
| Emberforge | 30-40 | +6 h | +20 h |
| Frostveil | 40-50 | +7 h | +25 h |
| Void Rift | 50-60 | +8 h | +30 h |
New realms open with a story gate + level; gear from the previous realm stays useful via upgrades and sockets.

## Gear power curve
- Item Power = base by rarity x realm tier + upgrades + socket bonuses.
- Each rarity step is about +25% power; each realm tier about +40%.
- A realm's Legendary roughly equals the next realm's Rare, so chasing Legendaries is optional but rewarding.
- Forge upgrades +0 to +10: cheap early, costly after +7; failures don't destroy the item (no rage-quit).

## Anti-frustration rules
- No hard grind walls: if a player is under-geared for the next step, the game points to 2-3 ways to catch up (dailies, Forge, Hard Rootvault).
- Pity on rolls and chests (already designed).
- Bad-luck protection on Relic Set drops (each run without a set piece increases the chance).
- Rest XP: 2x XP for a while after being offline 8h+ (capped).
- Level scaling in group content so friends of different levels can play together.

## Paid boosts (fair)
XP/luck/loot timed boosts speed things up but never unlock power that free players can't get. Mastery and gear are all earnable.

## Beta tuning targets
- Player reaches Haven by 5-6 min, Lv 5 by 20 min, first Rootvault Normal clear by 30 min, first Epic by about 1.5 h, first Legendary by 6-10 h (pity guarantees by about 1,000 rolls).
- Watch: where levels stall, % reaching Lv 10, D1/D7 retention.

## v1.1 (Kevin's notes): Rewarding grind, events, community boosts

### Grind that feels rewarding
- Slow the curve: beta Lv 20 target ~15-20 h (was 8-10 h); full game Lv 60 ~150 h+, plus endless Mastery.
- Reward density rule: something good every ~2-3 min (drop, chest, quest tick, bar fill), something exciting every ~15-20 min (Epic+, level-up, set piece), something huge every few hours (Legendary, new unlock).
- Visible bars everywhere: level, class, crafting, set completion, pity counter, Mastery. Players always see "almost there".
- Milestone rewards at every 5 levels (cosmetic + chest + title). Session-end recap screen: "This session: +3 levels, 2 Epics, 41% to your Relic Set".

### Events (return hooks + grind help)
- **Weekend XP/Loot rushes**: 2x XP or 2x drops Fri-Sun.
- **Shardstorms** (hourly, server-wide): shards rain in Haven/Verdant for 3 min; everyone grabs boosted rolls. Announced 2 min ahead.
- **World bosses**: every 2-3 h a realm boss spawns; everyone nearby gets loot (no last-hit stealing).
- **Seasonal events** (2-3 weeks): limited realm/biome, event currency, cosmetic track, event shards; Halloween/winter themes.
- **Community goals**: server-wide or global progress bar (e.g. "defeat 1M saplings") unlocks a 2x XP weekend for everyone.

### Patron Aura (whale perk that helps everyone)
Gamepass/subscription "Patron" (or timed "Blessing" boosts):
- Owner gets their own perks (cosmetic aura, +XP, VIP chest).
- **Everyone within ~60 studs (or in the same party/dungeon/server) gets +10% XP and +10% gold** while the Patron is nearby. Visible golden aura + "Blessed by <name>" buff icon.
- Server-wide **Blessings**: a spender can buy a 30-min "Shardstorm Blessing" that boosts the whole server (+25% XP/luck for all), with a server announcement thanking them ("<name> blessed the server!").
- Stacking caps: max +25% total from community boosts so free players benefit but nothing breaks.
- Luck-type server blessings must respect paid-random rules: in restricted regions, those players get the XP/gold part only, not luck.
- Why it works: whales get status and gratitude, free players want to party with them, everyone wins. Patrons naturally become community anchors.

### Community: helping each other
- **Helper rewards**: veterans who run lower-level dungeons with newer players get Mentor Tokens (cosmetics, titles) + full loot scaling.
- **Revive and assist credit**: reviving or healing allies grants bonus XP.
- **Shared kill credit and loot**: nobody steals drops; everyone in range gets their own loot.
- **Party bonus**: +5% XP per party member (max +15%).
- **"Kudos" system**: thank a helpful player after a run; weekly kudos leaders get a title/aura.
- Guild perks later amplify all of this.

## v1.2 additions
- **Welcome Back**: players away 14+ days get 2x XP for 3 h of play + a Rare Chest + a "what's new" card.
- **Prestige / Rebirth (post-launch)**: at max level, reset level for a permanent prestige badge, +2% XP/gold per prestige (capped), and exclusive cosmetics/auras. Gear, classes, and collections are kept.
- **Leaderboards**: Highest level/Mastery, Most Mythics, Fastest Rootvault per difficulty, Top Patrons, Most Kudos (weekly). Weekly top 10 get a title/aura.
- **XP boost hard cap**: all stacking multipliers (events, Patron, party, rest, paid boosts, prestige) cap at +100% total; the UI shows the current total and cap.
