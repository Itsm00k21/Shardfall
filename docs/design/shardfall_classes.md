# Shardfall Classes v1 (draft, Oct 16 2026)

Shared rules: 4 skills + 1 ultimate (fills from dealing/taking/healing damage). 1 core passive per class + 3 talent passives unlocked at class Lv 5/15/30 (pick 1 of 2 each). Unique class resource. Free class swap in Haven; each class levels separately. Shards modify skills (Fire = burn, Frost = slow, Void = curse, Tide = heal-on-hit, Verdant = shield-on-hit).

## 1. Warden - Tank (Verdant Wilds)
Resource: **Bark** (builds when hit, spent on shields)
Core passive - *Deep Roots*: -15% damage taken while standing still; nearby allies take -5%.
Skills:
1. Thorn Bash - shield strike, short stun, taunts the target.
2. Rootgrasp - roots enemies in a circle for 2s.
3. Barkskin - spend Bark for a shield = 20% max HP.
4. Vine Pull - yank an enemy (or a downed ally) to you.
Ultimate - **Heart of the Grove**: 6s, become a giant treant, taunt all nearby, 50% damage reduction, slams heal allies.
Talents: Lv5 Thornmail (reflect 10%) / Overgrowth (bigger roots). Lv15 Guardian (redirect 30% of an ally's damage to you) / Ironbark (+Bark gain). Lv30 Eternal Grove (cheat death once per run) / Wildheart (ult lasts +3s).

## 2. Tidecaller - Healer/Support (Sunken Reach)
Resource: **Tide** (rises while healing, ebbs over time; high tide = stronger heals)
Core passive - *Ebb & Flow*: overhealing becomes a small water shield.
Skills:
1. Tidal Bolt - damage an enemy or heal an ally (smart target).
2. Healing Pool - a zone that heals over 5s.
3. Bubble Ward - shields an ally and cleanses one debuff.
4. Undertow - wave that knocks enemies back and slows them.
Ultimate - **Monsoon**: rain for 8s, big party heal over time, revives one downed ally.
Talents: Lv5 Riptide (bolt chains) / Still Waters (pool +50% size). Lv15 Lifeline (tether to an ally, share healing) / Pressure (Undertow stuns). Lv30 Tsunami (ult also damages) / Deep Current (Tide never drops below 50%).

## 3. Forgeborn - Bruiser (Emberforge)
Resource: **Heat** (builds with hits; at max = Overheat, +damage but drains HP slowly)
Core passive - *Tempered*: +1% damage per 10% missing HP (max +8%).
Skills:
1. Hammerfall - big overhead slam, small AoE.
2. Molten Charge - dash forward leaving fire.
3. Anvil Guard - block for 1.5s; a perfect block refunds Heat.
4. Eruption - vent all Heat into a ring of fire; damage scales with Heat.
Ultimate - **Forgeheart**: 8s, permanent Overheat with no HP drain, every 3rd hit erupts.
Talents: Lv5 Slag (fire slows) / Bellows (+Heat gain). Lv15 Unbreakable (CC immune while Overheated) / Smelt (Eruption heals). Lv30 Meteor Hammer (Hammerfall calls a meteor) / Cinder Soul (ult refunds on kill).

## 4. Frostblade - Agile Melee (Frostveil)
Resource: **Chill** stacks on enemies (5 stacks = Frozen 1.5s)
Core passive - *Shatter*: +50% crit damage vs. Frozen; breaking a freeze deals bonus AoE.
Skills:
1. Ice Edge - fast 3-hit combo, applies Chill.
2. Glacial Dash - dash through enemies, 1 Chill each; 2 charges.
3. Frost Mirror - leave a decoy that taunts briefly then explodes.
4. Cold Snap - instantly apply 2 Chill in a cone.
Ultimate - **Absolute Zero**: freeze everything nearby; for 5s your crits are guaranteed vs. Frozen.
Talents: Lv5 Biting Wind (+speed on freeze) / Permafrost (Frozen +0.5s). Lv15 Twin Mirrors (2 decoys) / Avalanche (Dash 3 charges). Lv30 Winter's Edge (kills spread Chill) / Glacier (ult also shields you).

## 5. Voidcaster - Ranged DPS (Void Rift)
Resource: **Void** (spend for spells; at 0, you are Unstable: +20% damage, take +20%)
Core passive - *Entropy*: curses tick 15% faster on enemies below 50% HP.
Skills:
1. Void Bolt - piercing ranged bolt.
2. Rift - a portal zone that pulls enemies in and damages over time.
3. Blink - short teleport; leaves a curse where you were.
4. Hex of Unmaking - curse: the target takes +15% damage from everyone.
Ultimate - **Event Horizon**: a black hole for 5s, pulls and crushes, detonates at the end.
Talents: Lv5 Echo (bolt repeats 30%) / Hollow (Rift +size). Lv15 Phase Shift (Blink 2 charges) / Contagion (Hex spreads on death). Lv30 Singularity (ult +50% pull) / Void Pact (Unstable has no downside for 5s after a kill).

## Raid role coverage
Tank: Warden (Forgeborn off-tank). Heal: Tidecaller. Melee DPS: Frostblade, Forgeborn. Ranged DPS: Voidcaster.

## Beta scope
All 5 classes, 4 skills + core passive. Ultimates + Lv5 talent in beta if time allows; Lv15/30 talents post-beta.
