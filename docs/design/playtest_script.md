# Playtest script

E-005. One fresh profile, on a phone. About 15 minutes. The path is spawn, the plaza, the first dungeon, the first loot, and the first crack, inside about a minute, then a short look at the bag, Settings, the Forge, and the Gold shop.

Feel and looks only. Readability and phone feel. Leave gold totals, odds math, and damage numbers out of the notes.

Write the build, the device, and the viewport. Rotate the phone at the Forge step. If the screen is not exactly 390×844 or 844×390, use the closest portrait and landscape and write the real size.

Do not spend Robux. If a purchase prompt appears, stop and write the label.

This pass is the phone. A controller check comes later.

Raids, Trading, the Market Board, pet evolution, boss pets, Shard Pass, salvage, the dust shop, and the daily boss stay out of this pass. Confirm they are hidden. Do not open them to try them.

The shots in `docs/design/vision/refs/` are reference only. Do not look for their art, their names, or a paid offer. There is no premium currency.

## Steps

### 1. Spawn

You join on a new profile. Read the cold open, or tap Skip intro, so you are standing in the plaza with time left for the rest of the minute. The line you read is short, and Skip intro is easy to see.

You can read two counters, Gold and Shards. Shards shows 10. The portrait, the HP bar, and those counters sit apart. Nothing on the screen asks for Robux.

Pass: You are in the plaza, Shards reads 10, and the counters sit off the portrait.

### 2. Plaza and one nav

You stand where you spawned and look.

The left rail reads Hero, Bag, Pets, Roll, Shop, and Settings. Open Bag. The panel has a title and a Close control, and the page fills the panel. There is no second column of Hero, Bag, Pets, Roll, Shop, or Settings inside it. The rail stays on screen, and that rail is how you change pages. Close the panel from Close.

The Rootvault gate is readable from where you stand. The portrait, the HP bar, the goal tracker, the rail, and the action buttons do not cover each other. On a short landscape phone the rail sits clear of the HP bar and the portrait. The action buttons are easy to tap.

Raids, Trading, the Market Board, pet evolution, boss pets, and Shard Pass are absent from the rail and from the panel.

Pass: One rail, no tab column inside the panel, and those parked pages are absent.

### 3. First fight

You walk to Rootvault and go in. The gate name was clear before you entered. The first room reads as a fight.

Tap Atk on an enemy in front of you. It answers right away.

Tap Heavy once, still facing that enemy. You do not hold the button. The swing shows, and the enemy reacts after a short beat, about a third of a second.

Turn so that enemy is behind your back and swing again. The enemy behind you does not react. Face it and swing once more. It reacts.

Dodge and Jump are reachable and do not cover the enemy.

Pass: Heavy connects in front after that short beat, and a swing does not hit an enemy behind you.

### 4. First loot

You fight until something drops, and you read it.

The name and the rarity color are readable. You can tell what you picked up. The card does not cover the fight, and it does not cover the action buttons. Nothing about the drop asks for Robux.

Pass: You can read the drop, and the card stays off the buttons.

### 5. Bag tip and Equip

Open Bag from the rail. The loot from this run is there.

Press a card and keep your finger on it. A box appears: the name uses the rarity color, each stat is its own line, and under that a compare line or a line that nothing is equipped in that slot. The box stays inside the screen.

Tap Equip on that card. The button reads Equipped right away.

Salvage is absent from this bag.

Pass: The tip is readable and on screen, Equip reads Equipped at once, and Salvage is absent.

### 6. First crack

Open Roll from the rail. From the moment you could move, this first result should land inside about a minute.

The panel is readable: a crack button, a shard count, pity rows, and odds lines in one clear format. You crack once. The shard shakes, a crack spreads, then the result bursts in and the name is readable. Rare and above hold longer before that burst. If reduced motion is on, that reveal stays short. A tap during the motion skips to the result and does not spend a second shard.

The panel stays open. You crack one more time. It stays open the whole time. A tap on the dark backdrop from the same press that cracked does not close it. A later tap on the backdrop still closes it.

Epic and Legendary each have a row. Pity and a count sit on the right, with the lock beside the count. The middle of the row does not cover the lock or the count.

After each crack, the shard number on the panel is the same number as the Shards counter.

Pass: Two cracks, the panel stayed up through them, the name was readable, and the two shard numbers match.

### 7. Settings and pets

Open Settings from the rail. The title reads Settings. Sound, text size, and the other live rows can be there. Leave them as they are. Trades, Raid invites, and hotkeys named raids and market are absent. Close it.

Open Pets. Evolution and a boss pet are absent. Close it.

Pass: Settings has no Trades row, no Raid invites row, and no raids or market hotkey. Pets has no evolution and no boss pet.

### 8. Forge sheet

Walk to the Forge in the plaza and open it. A line says Forge. Upgrade gear. Write down the words on the world prompt.

On a portrait phone, 390×844, scroll the sheet until an upgrade row is fully readable. Rotate to landscape, 844×390, and scroll again. The sheet misses the HP bar and the action buttons.

Salvage is absent. Leave Forge Boost alone. Leave a Chests button alone if you see one. A Materials tab, and a Craft label at the top of the sheet, can be on screen. Write them down and keep going.

Pass: An upgrade row is fully readable on both 390×844 and 844×390, and Salvage is absent.

### 9. Gold shop

Open Shop from the rail, read the offers, and close it.

It reads as a Gold shop: a Healing Tonic and a bag slot, with prices in Gold. A pet slot is absent. Dust shop is absent. No price asks for Robux, and no purchase prompt appears. The Market Board stays hidden.

Pass: The offers are the Healing Tonic and the bag slot. A pet slot, Dust shop, and a Robux price are absent.

### 10. Still hidden

From the plaza, look once more. Across this whole pass, salvage, a dust shop, and a daily boss stayed hidden. You never saw a control that starts a daily boss. Do not go looking for a way to start one.

Pass: Salvage, the dust shop, and the daily boss stayed hidden.

## Write down

The build, the device, and the viewport. Whether the first crack felt inside about a minute. The words on the Forge prompt, and any Materials tab or Craft label on that sheet. The one place text was hard to read, or a purchase prompt if one appeared.
