# Shardfall Vision (UI + World)

Kevin's look target, from reference shots of other games (Oct 17, 2026). **References only.** Never copy their art, names, or pay-to-win offers. Shardfall has no premium currency, nothing costs Robux, and any season pass track is free and earned.

Images are in `docs/design/vision/refs/`.

## Priority
1. UI fixes and gameplay first. Every button, key and tab must do something (see the click harness from #213).
2. Menu readability and style (this doc).
3. World and lighting later (T-148 lighting, T-162 nameplates, both deferred).

## Menus (`ref1-6`, `mock_roll`, `mock_bag`, `mock_pets`)
- Dark rounded panels, bold white text with a dark stroke, high contrast.
- List on the left and detail pane on the right, with one big primary button.
- Rarity-bordered item tiles with level badges and upgrade arrows.
- Pill category tabs and a red CLOSE button top-right.
- Roll/chest rows show cost, x1/x10 buttons (no x100 unless the economy supports it), odds bars and pity bars.
- Labeled nav icons with badges, plus a What's New panel.
- Bag has Equip Best and a Capacity readout.
- Pet rolls and pet-shard cracking get their own easy-to-find place. Keep the original "crack a shard" reveal.
- Ignore the blue gem currency in the mockups.

## HUD (`hub2_*`, `hud1`)
- WoW-style 1-8 action bar at the bottom center: slim, key letters on each slot, holds skills, potions and items, click or keypress.
- Quest objectives as a compact tracker top-left, and round icon buttons top-right.
- Round sword, dodge and jump buttons bottom-right on mobile.
- Currency column on the left edge, a big boss HP bar top-center during fights, and split weapon and passive bars with lock slots.

## Season pass (`pass1`)
- Two reward rows with tier hexes between them, an XP bar with an Earn XP button, and Claimed/Locked tags.
- **Shardfall version: free, earned track only.** No Robux unlock, tier skips or gifting for currency.

## World (`world1-2`, `hub2_*`), visuals pass later
- Simple game art: chunky low-poly trees, flat bright colors, blocky castles, tiled floors, soft distance fog, clean even lighting, clear sky.
- Calm background so the busy UI stands out. It looks good because of bold shapes and clean light, not detail.
- One compact plaza hub. Each destination (Roll, Forge, Pets, Dungeons, Raid) is a labeled building or arch portal around a central fountain or bonfire, with big signs or floating gold-trimmed labels (these replace the current nameplates).
- Dungeon gates consolidated into a clean, uncluttered plaza.
- Patterned tile floors, colored carpet paths, lamp posts, small planters, banners on gates, leaderboard boards.
