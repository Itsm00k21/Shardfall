## Where the numbers live

| topic | live source |
|---|---|
| Controls | `Keybinds.COMBAT`, `Keybinds.MENU`, `Keybinds.GAMEPAD`, `Keybinds.SPRINT`. Menu letters can be rebound. Combat keys cannot. |
| Red and blue | `CombatFeel` red verb `dodge`, blue verb `interrupt`. |
| Tokens | One Shard Token per crack (`Currencies`). Prism Tokens are the bought wallet. |
| x2 luck | `RarityConfig.BONUS_EVERY` is 10. The ready label is `x2 Luck Ready`. |
| Odds labels | `RarityConfig.oneIn`: Common 2, Mythic 25000, at x1 before luck. |
| Armory pity | Epic 100, Legendary 1000. The next crack is the guarantee at 99/100 and 999/1000. |
| Chest pity | Rare chests 20 to Epic or better. Epic chests 15 to Legendary. Separate counters. |
| Raids | Story and Normal are level 15. Story pays 25 marks, Normal 40, weekly room 600. Student is 200. |
| Wings | Thornwarden Twins, Mother Sapwell, Galecrown, Grandmother Gnarl Awakened. |
| Practice | `RaidPractice.rewards` returns empty loot. |
| Pets | An egg, or 50 gold. Affinity 5. Bond 5. Feed is 25 XP. A level is 50 XP, cap 50. Combos cap at 3. |
| Craft | Healing Tonic is 1 Spore Cap and 1 Living Vine, and restores 40% HP. Briar Edge is 4 Verdant Wood and 2 Living Vine. |
| Keys | Uncommon chest 40, Rare 150, Epic 600. Legendary and Mythic chests are not sold. |

Flags: ButtonX is the live gamepad heavy key. Pity staying outside the odds line is the live notice, not a second roll. No other game's tip line is used.
