# Weapons batch 1

G-001. Thirty new weapons, five types across all six rarities, for a later catalog draft and the 3D art pass.

One sword, dagger, hammer, staff, and bow at each rarity. Greatswords are in batch 2. Combat ranges stay on the `ItemBases.luau` row for that type. These numbers are not live. Names are not in `ItemBases.luau`.
