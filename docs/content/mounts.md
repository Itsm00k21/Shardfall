# Mounts batch 1

G-024. Fifteen mounts for a later catalog and the 3D art pass.

Cosmetic only. No combat power, no luck, no drop odds, no Raid Marks. Not a Robux price list.

Choice: every rarity uses the same Haven pace, the player's walk. Rarity is silhouette and glow. A mount is not ridden in a fight or a dungeon. Suggested rule: swinging or entering a crawl dismounts you. Fast travel stays free.
