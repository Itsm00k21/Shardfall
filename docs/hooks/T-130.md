# T-130 gold retune, proposal only

No live number changes in this row. `Economy.luau`, `Dungeons.luau`, `DungeonService.luau`, and `LoopService.luau` stay as they are until Kevin accepts this.

## For Kevin

Forging one item to +10 still costs 90,794 gold. A Rootvault clear would pay 360 instead of 45, so an engaged player reaches +10 in about 200 clears instead of about 2,000.

A finished daily or weekly would pay 150 instead of 40. Gold Find stays a 5–15% bonus on a Rare or better piece, and the forge curve stays 500 then about ×1.6.

Starter gold stays 250, so the kit still cannot buy the first upgrade. Chests stay 40, 150, and 600 Shard Keys. Nothing in the game changes until you say yes.

## Why these knobs

Live forge cost is `floor(500 * 1.6 ^ level + 0.5)` in `Economy.upgradeCost`. The ten steps sum to 90,794. A Rootvault clear pays 45 (`Dungeons.luau`), so clears alone take 2,018 runs for one item. The audit in `docs/design/economy_audit.md` (G-027, #121) keeps that curve and raises a clear to 250. 90,794 / 250 is still 364 clears, outside 150–250, and dailies at 40 gold do not close the gap. This proposal keeps the curve and moves the clear, the daily, and nothing else.

Gold Find stays `floor(base * goldFind + 0.5)` (`CombatMath.bonusGold`). A trait rolls 5–15% (`Traits.luau`). Common and Uncommon have no trait slot (`Rarities.TRAIT_SLOTS`).

Clear gold, raid gold, wave gold, and the treasure door are all ×8, so a raid stays ahead of a dungeon and a door stays the same share of a clear (200 / 360, same as 25 / 45). Wave 1 pays 88. Wave 10 pays 304. Wave 20 pays 544.

The finish pay is the `40` in `LoopService` that runs for a daily and a weekly, because both lists share that grant. 150 is short of one forge step. Three dailies are 450, still under the 500 for +1.

## Engaged player

Ten Rootvault clears a day. Day 1 has no Gold Find. From the next clear on, one trait at 10% (the middle of 5–15%), so a clear pays 360 + 36 = 396. Each day finishes 3 dailies. Each week finishes 5 weeklies. The starter kit's 250 is included once. Pass tier 1 pays its 150 when the XP gets there. Tier 20's 400 is later than this climb.

Chapter 1 quest gold is that same 250, so it is not added again. The side-quest list in `docs/content/quests_side.md` sums to 442 and is not wired, so it is not in the count. The grove hand-in stays 15.

Engaged +10 is clear 203, on day 21. Rootvault gold alone, with no kit and no dailies, is 253 clears. With 10% Gold Find and no dailies, it is 230. A 15% trait from the first clear, with the dailies, is 195. Six clears a day with the 10% trait is 186. Twelve clears a day is 207. Those sit inside 150–250. The 253 is the path with no dailies and no Gold Find.

Hour 1 is the 250 kit, 4 clears at 360, and 1 daily at 150. Day 1 is the kit, 10 clears at 360, and 3 dailies at 150. Pass XP that day is 700, under one tier. Week 1 is the kit, 10 clears at 360, 60 clears at 396, 21 dailies, 5 weeklies, and the tier-1 150. That week's XP is 5,400, tier 5, and the free track pays gold only at tier 1.

A later hour, once Gold Find is on, at 8 clears is 3,168 gold. The design draft's 8–12k an hour (`shardfall_economy.md`) would need a much larger clear, and that would finish +10 in under 150 clears. This proposal hits the clear count. The hour target can move after a Rootvault clear has a measured time.

## Checks

Starter gold stays 250. The first step stays 500, so the kit cannot buy +1. One proposed clear plus the kit is 610, which buys +1 and leaves 110, short of +2 (1,300). Five eggs still cost 250 and still leave the player short of +1.

Chest prices stay Uncommon 40, Rare 150, Epic 600 Shard Keys (`ChestConfig.KEY_SHOP`). Legendary and Mythic stay unsold. Those prices are keys, and this proposal does not spend gold on them.

Auto-farm stays 40% and still stops at 2 hours. Salvage still pays materials, not gold. Gold still has no cap. At this clear size the audit's worry about 8–12k an hour with no cap stays put. +8 to +10 materials stay on T-131 (`gnarl_seed`). This row does not change them.

Schema stays 23. No Robux.
